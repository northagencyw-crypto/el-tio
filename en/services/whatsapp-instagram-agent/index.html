An AI agent that answers your WhatsApp and Instagram

It replies in seconds at any hour with your business information, books appointments, takes down details and saves every conversation in your CRM. When a person is needed, it hands the conversation over with the context already written.

What he does

Most of the messages a business gets are repeats: the hours, the price, whether there's room, how to get there. The agent answers those messages in your words and with your information, and frees your team for the ones that need someone.

  • Answers with the information you give it: hours, prices, policies, location, FAQ.
  • Books reservations, visits, trial classes or calls on your calendar.
  • Qualifies: asks the questions your best salesperson would ask before passing the contact along.
  • Saves every contact in the CRM with its conversation, so nobody gets lost in an employee's phone.
  • Hands off to a person anything that's a complaint, an exception or a big sale, with a written summary.

The rules it follows

WhatsApp treats two situations differently. When a customer messages you, a 24-hour window opens in which the agent can reply freely. To message someone outside that window you have to use approved templates, and the person has to have agreed to receive messages from your business.[1] The WhatsApp Business policy also requires you to respect anyone who asks to stop receiving them.[2]

On Instagram, the agent answers direct messages from people who write to you. It doesn't go out and message accounts that never wrote to you.

Since it saves names and phone numbers, it handles personal data. In Mexico that requires a privacy notice that says what data you keep and why; the agent can send the link in its first message. The chatbot guide for restaurants explains it in more detail.

FAQ

Can I use the WhatsApp number I already have?

Almost always, yes. The agent runs on the WhatsApp Business API, which needs a connected number. In the Audit we check whether it makes sense to move your current number or use a new one, and what happens to your chat history in each case.

Does the agent say it's an AI?

Yes, in its first message. People who just want a reservation or a price don't mind, and anyone who'd rather talk to a person can ask for one at any time.

What happens when it doesn't know the answer?

It doesn't make things up. It takes down the details, alerts the right person on your team and hands over the conversation with the context written out. Every week we review what it couldn't answer and add it to its information.

Does it answer in English?

Yes. It answers in whatever language people write in, with the same information. That's especially useful for hotels, vacation rentals and real estate agencies with foreign buyers.

Can it message my customers to offer them something?

Only people who agreed to receive messages from your business, and only with templates approved by WhatsApp. The agent doesn't send cold messages: besides going against WhatsApp's policy, it's the fastest way to get your number blocked.

Can I cancel?

Yes. It's month to month, with no long contract. The contacts and conversations stay in your name.

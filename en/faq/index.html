The Audit

Is the Audit really free?

Yes. It's a 15 to 25 minute questionnaire and a map you get within 24 to 48 business hours. We don't ask for a card and the map is yours, whether you hire us or not.

What does the Audit ask?

How your business works today, area by area: sales, operations, data, marketing, customer service, team and the tools you use. The questions change depending on your industry.

Who reviews it?

A person, before it reaches you. The map doesn't come straight out of a machine.

What do I get?

A map of what's worth optimizing and automating, in what order and why. If what would help you most isn't something we do, we tell you that too.

Pricing and payments

What's the USD 49 call for?

It's half an hour to go over the map with a person, answer your questions and leave with a proposal. If you sign up for a package within the next 7 days, the USD 49 comes off the setup fee. It's optional: you can sign up without it.

Is there a minimum contract?

No. The packages are month to month and you can cancel anytime. What we set up and the data stay in your name.

When does the timeline start?

The 10, 14 or 21 business days, depending on the package, start when we have your business information and access to your channels. The proposal lists exactly what we need.

What's not included?

Third-party costs that depend on your usage, like the template messages WhatsApp charges for or a phone line for the voice agent. They're itemized in the proposal before we start.

Why does it say “from”?

Because that's the price of a normal site for a business. If your requirements are complicated, like lots of pages, copy that has to be written from scratch or integrations, it can cost a bit more and we tell you in writing before we start.

Can I pay in pesos?

Prices are in US dollars. The written proposal states the amount and the payment method.

Setup

What do you need from me?

Your business information (hours, prices, policies, common questions), access to your channels and a contact person who can answer questions during setup.

How do I know it works before my customers use it?

Before we hand it over, we test it end to end with two phones, as if we were customers. Your team gets a short guide to run it.

Do you have to come to my business?

No need. The Audit is online, the call is by video and the setup is done remotely. If something needs to be seen in person, we agree on it in the proposal.

Does it work with what I already use?

Almost always. We work on top of the tools your business already uses when they can be connected. If you don't have a CRM, we set one up in your name.

What countries do you work in?

We're starting with Mexico: Mexico City, Monterrey, Guadalajara and the Riviera Maya. Since everything is done remotely, the Audit already works for businesses in any country in Latin America.

Data and rules

Who owns the data?

You do. The contacts, the conversations and whatever gets set up stay in your name, with your logins. We don't use your data for other clients.

Does the agent say it's an AI?

Yes. The WhatsApp agent says so in its first message, and the voice agent at the start of the call.

Can you send mass WhatsApp messages to my contact list?

Only to people who agreed to receive messages from your business, with templates approved by WhatsApp. We don't send cold WhatsApp messages: it goes against their policy and puts your number at risk.

Do you promise results?

We don't promise improvement percentages. We measure what matters in your business before and after the setup, and show you what changed.

How we're starting in Monterrey

Monterrey has a large base of manufacturing, distribution and business services. In that kind of business the bottleneck is rarely getting the order: it's responding to it. Hours go by between the customer sending the list and someone getting back with a price, and in that time the customer asks another supplier.

That's why in Monterrey El Tío starts on the sales side: quoting faster, following up on every quote and letting the sales team sell instead of copying data from one file to another.

FAQ

Do you have to come to my company?

No need. The Audit is online, the review call is by video and the setup is tested end to end remotely. If a project needs on-site work, we agree on it in the proposal.

Does it connect to my inventory or invoicing system?

If the system has a way to get data out (an API, an export or an email that always arrives in the same format), yes. We check it in the Audit before quoting. That work is quoted in writing.

How much does it cost?

The Audit is free. After that there are three packages: Website (from USD 300 setup and USD 39 a month), Website + chatbot (from USD 400 setup and USD 59 a month) and Website + chatbot + prospecting (from USD 500 setup and USD 79 a month). Anything beyond that is quoted in writing. Everything is month to month, no long contract.

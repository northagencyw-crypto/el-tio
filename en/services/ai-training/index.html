Artificial intelligence training for company teams

Your team learns to use AI in their everyday work, with their own cases, and comes away with clear rules about what can be done with your customers' data.

Take the free Audit Message us on WhatsApp

Why now

Your team is already using artificial intelligence, whether you know it or not. Each person with their own tool, their personal account and their own judgment. The risk is that someone pastes your customer list or a contract into a public tool without knowing where that information ends up.

Training puts that in order. Everyone uses the same thing, the same way, and knows where the line is.

What it looks like

  • By department: sales, customer service, operations or administration, each with its real tasks and not textbook examples.
  • With material that sticks around: how-to guides for each task that the team keeps using afterward.
  • With written rules: what data can be used, in which tools, and who approves what goes out to a customer.
  • With follow-up: we check what's being used and what isn't, and adjust.

Who it's for

For companies with a team that don't want to implement anything yet and would rather start by understanding it. And for clients who already have something set up and want their team to run it without depending on anyone.

The format, the number of people and the departments are set in writing before we start.

FAQ

Is it a prompt-writing course?

No. We work on the real tasks of each department in your company. Everyone comes away knowing which part of their job they can do with AI, with which tool and with what precautions.

Do we need to have implemented something first?

No. Many companies start here, so the team understands what's possible before deciding what to implement.

How much does it cost?

It depends on how many people and how many departments. It's quoted in writing after a conversation.

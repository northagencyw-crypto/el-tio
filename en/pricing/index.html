How the whole process works

The terms

  • Month to month. No long contract and no cancellation penalty.
  • Your data is yours. Contacts, conversations and everything we set up stay in your name, with your own logins.
  • Timelines start once we have your business information and access to your channels.
  • Third-party costs that depend on your usage, like WhatsApp template messages or a phone line, are itemized in the proposal.
  • No percentage promises. We measure before and after, and show you what changed.

FAQ

Is the Audit really free?

Yes. It's a 15 to 25 minute questionnaire and a map you get within 24 to 48 business hours. We don't ask for a card and the map is yours, whether you hire us or not.

What's the USD 49 call for?

It's half an hour to go over the map with a person, answer your questions and leave with a proposal. If you sign up for a package within the next 7 days, the USD 49 comes off the setup fee. It's optional: you can sign up without it.

Is there a minimum contract?

No. The packages are month to month and you can cancel anytime. What we set up and the data stay in your name.

When do the 7 or 21 days start?

Once we have your business information and access to your channels. The proposal lists exactly what we need.

What's not included?

Third-party costs that depend on your usage, like the template messages WhatsApp charges for or a phone line for the voice agent. They're itemized in the proposal before we start.

Can I pay in pesos?

Prices are in US dollars. The written proposal states the amount and the payment method.

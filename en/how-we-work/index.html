How we work

First we look, then we set things up

El Tío audits, optimizes, automates and trains, in that order. Before setting anything up we look at how your business works today, because automating a messy process just makes it faster.

Take the free Audit Message us on WhatsApp

From the Audit to delivery

  1. Audit

    You fill out a guided 15 to 25 minute questionnaire on sales, operations, data, marketing, customer service, team and tools. A person reviews it, and within 24 to 48 business hours you get the map: what to do, in what order and why. It's free.

  2. Questions by chat

    If something in the map is unclear, you ask on WhatsApp or Instagram and we answer in writing.

  3. Written proposal

    The right package, what it includes in your case, what we need from you and the delivery date. Nothing starts without this.

  4. Payment and kickoff form

    If the proposal works for you, you pay it with a payment link and fill in a short form with your business information and access to your channels.

  5. Setup

    We start from your industry's template and fill it with your information: the website, the agent and the CRM, and prospecting, depending on the package. We connect your channels and your calendar.

  6. End-to-end testing

    Before we hand it over, we test it with two phones as if we were customers: send a message, book, get the reminder, pay, show up in the report.

  7. Handover and a guide for your team

    Your team gets a short guide: where to see conversations, when to step in and how to ask for changes. The website is delivered in 10 business days, with the chatbot in 14 and with prospecting in 21.

  8. Month to month

    We review what the agent couldn't answer, adjust for whatever changed in your business and go over the numbers with you.

House rules

Scope in writing

What gets set up, how long it takes and what we need from you, agreed on before we start.

Month to month

No long contracts. Cancel anytime.

Your data is yours

Everything stays in your name, with your logins. Nothing is shared with other clients.

The AI says it's an AI

The WhatsApp agent and the voice agent say so at the start of every conversation.

On top of what you already use

We don't sell a platform of our own. We work on top of your tools when they can be connected.

A person answers you

On WhatsApp, in Spanish or English, not a ticket system.

What we don't do

  • We don't promise results or publish improvement percentages.
  • We don't make up clients, testimonials or case studies.
  • We don't send cold WhatsApp messages to anyone, in your name or ours.
  • We don't quote an implementation before we measure.

What we need from you

  • Your business information: hours, prices, policies and the questions you get most.
  • Access to your channels: WhatsApp, Instagram, calendar and, if you have one, your CRM.
  • Someone on your team who can answer questions during setup.

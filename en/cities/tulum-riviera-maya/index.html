How we're starting in the Riviera Maya

In the Riviera Maya, a good share of customers come from another country, write in English and make decisions from another time zone. Many bookings go through platforms that take a commission and keep the guest's contact info: the next year you pay again for the same person.

El Tío works in the region so that every inquiry gets answered in the language it comes in, the booking goes to the direct channel when possible and the guest's history stays in your database.

FAQ

Does the agent answer in English?

Yes. It answers in whatever language people write in, with the same information about your business.

Does it connect to my booking system or channel manager?

It depends on the system. If it has a way to connect, the agent can check availability or enter the booking. If not, it takes the details and alerts whoever confirms. We look at it in the Audit.

Do you have to come to my hotel?

No need. The whole process can be done remotely, and it's tested with two phones before we hand it over. If something needs to be seen in person, we agree on it in the proposal.

How much does it cost?

The Audit is free. After that there are three packages: El Tío contesta (USD 490 setup and USD 149 a month), El Tío ordena (USD 1,500 setup and USD 390 a month) and El Tío resuelve (from USD 4,000). Everything is month to month, with no long contract.

The problem

In most businesses, customers are scattered: in the WhatsApp on each salesperson's phone, in a spreadsheet, in the Instagram inbox and in someone's head. While that person is around, it works. When they go on vacation or leave the company, the customers leave with them.

A CRM is the one list of who contacted you, what they want, where things stand and what's still left to do. It works if your team uses it, and your team uses it if it saves them work instead of adding to it.

What we set up

  • An organized contact list: one contact per person, no duplicates, with where they came from (an ad, Instagram, a referral) so you know which channel brings in customers.
  • Your industry's pipeline: stages with names your team already uses, and every contact in the right one.
  • Reminders: for appointments, showings, reservations and pending calls, for the customer and for your team.
  • Collections in sequence: the payment reminder goes out on its own, with the wording you approve, until the payment is logged.
  • The weekly report: new contacts, replied to, booked, closed and awaiting payment, every Monday.

FAQ

I already have a CRM. Do I have to switch?

Not just for the sake of switching. If your team already uses it and it can be connected, we work on top of it. In the Audit we look at whether it makes sense to connect it or replace it, and we tell you why.

Who owns the data?

You do. The CRM is in your name, with your logins, and you can export your contacts whenever you want.

What happens to the contacts I have in Excel or on my phone?

We move them into the CRM and organize them: one contact per person, no duplicates, with where they came from and their stage. It's part of the setup.

Does collections send messages to my customers without me seeing them?

The reminders go out with wording you approve ahead of time, in the order and on the days you set. You see every message sent in the CRM and you can stop a customer's sequence at any time.

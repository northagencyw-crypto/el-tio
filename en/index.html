IN PRACTICE

Real automations

Examples of the kind of systems El Tío leaves running inside a business.

SALES

Instant replies on WhatsApp

Every inquiry gets an answer in seconds, it's qualified automatically, and the ones worth pursuing get booked on the salesperson's calendar, with a reminder for both sides.

MARKETING

Content that posts itself

The system writes the copy, creates the images and posts to social media every day, on a set calendar and schedule. Your team just keeps an eye on it.

CAMPAIGNS

Controlled drip broadcasts

WhatsApp campaigns sent in spaced-out batches, with calibrated pauses and send times, to protect your number and reach your whole list without getting blocked.

ADMINISTRATION

Collections without the chasing

The invoice goes out when the sale closes, it sends itself, and payment reminders go out in sequence until you're paid. Everything is logged.

MANAGEMENT

On Monday, the report is already there

Every week, management gets an automatic summary with sales, costs, variances and alerts, pulled from the systems the business already uses.

OPERATIONS

Inventory that warns you ahead of time

Stock is projected from historical demand and flags what to restock before you run out, with purchase orders ready to approve.

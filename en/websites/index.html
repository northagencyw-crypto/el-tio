Why your own domain

An Instagram profile, a Facebook page or a link-in-bio page belong to the platform. They're good for getting followers, but the rules, the reach and the way they show you change without notice. A site on your domain is yours: you decide what it says, you can move it to another provider, and it doesn't depend on an algorithm for someone to open it.

It's also what Google and AI assistants read when someone asks about a business like yours. If your information only lives in an Instagram photo or a PDF, it's hard for a machine to understand it and cite it.

Why eight languages

If you serve tourists, foreign buyers or customers from other countries, some of the people looking for you search in their own language. A site that's only in Spanish doesn't show up in those searches.

Google recommends a separate URL for each language, instead of switching the language with cookies or based on the browser, and asks you not to redirect people from one language to another automatically.[1] Each version is tagged with hreflang so Google understands they're the same page in different languages and shows the right one.[2] That's how we build it.

FAQ

Is the domain in my name?

Yes. The domain is registered to your business and the account is yours. If you ever stop working with us, you keep the site and the domain.

What languages does it include?

Eight, which we choose with you based on where your customers come from. Spanish and English are almost always in; the other six depend on your industry and your city. Each language has its own URL, so Google shows each person the right version.

Are the translations automatic?

They're done with the help of AI and a person reviews them before they go live, with your prices, your hours and your product names written correctly in each language.

I already have a website. Do you rebuild it?

First we review it in the Audit. If it works, we clean it up and add the languages and the optimization. If it makes more sense to start over, we explain why before we touch anything.

How long until I show up on Google?

Google says crawling a page can take anywhere from a few days to a few weeks.[6] Ranking high for a search takes longer, and no one can guarantee the top spot.[5] What we can do is make sure the site is built right from day one and track its progress every month.

Can I change the text myself?

Yes. We set you up to edit hours, prices, photos and news without depending on us. Big structural changes we do together so we don't break your rankings.

Best WhatsApp chatbots for restaurants in Tulum 2027: how to choose

The best WhatsApp chatbot for restaurants in Tulum in 2027 is the one that replies within minutes, at any hour and in the customer's language, using only the information you approved (reservations, takeout orders and menu questions), hands over to a person what it cannot solve and keeps every conversation in a CRM owned by the business. No single brand is best for everyone: there are five criteria to compare, listed below in order.

Why it matters in 2027

Large companies have already moved: in McKinsey's global survey published in November 2025, 88% of organizations said they regularly use AI in at least one business function.[1] Most small businesses, on the other hand, have not started yet. Criteria to compare

  1. Response speed, day and night. Measure how long it takes to answer a message that arrives at 11 p.m. Someone who waits until the next day has already written to other restaurants in Tulum. Harvard Business Review found that companies that responded to a lead within the first hour were far more likely to qualify it than those that took longer.[2]
  2. It answers in the customer's language. Many of your diners write in English, Spanish, French or Portuguese. Test it in at least two languages and check that it keeps the same facts in each.
  3. It uses your information and does not make things up. Ask to see how it handles questions about reservations, takeout orders and menu questions. It should use only what you loaded and say «let me pass you to the team» when it does not know.
  4. It hands over to a person at the right moment. A good chatbot does not trap anyone. It spots the complaint, the special case or the client ready to close and passes it to your team with the conversation's context.
  5. Every conversation lands in your CRM, within the rules. If chats live only on one phone, they leave when that team member leaves. The data must belong to the business and be exportable. Outside the 24-hour window you can only write with approved templates and with the customer's opt-in,[3] and in Mexico the 2025 personal data law applies.[4]

How to use them

Test each option with three real messages from your diners: one by day, one at night and one tricky one, in two languages. Note what happened with each criterion. The option that meets all five is the best one for your restaurant, even if it is not the best known.

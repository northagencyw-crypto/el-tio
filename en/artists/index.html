A2THE MAP

Where the real people are, city by city.

A list of your own with city, country and contact, and the map on top of it. The argument about whether to open a market stops: you look at where there are already people who said yes, and you build the route from that.

The artist provides the incentive: an unreleased track, a presale before anyone else, a private set, guestlist. Without an incentive you capture nothing, and we say so in the first meeting. This is a system set up on tools that already exist, not a platform of ours.

A3THE OWNED LIST

Four things, and none of them is a trick.

  1. The incentive

    Nobody leaves their email without getting something in return, and the artist chooses what to offer. You test one, measure how many it captured, and if it doesn't work you change the incentive, not the system.

  2. The permission

    Contacts are captured with explicit consent and it gets recorded. A list without permission isn't an asset, it's a problem.

  3. The city

    Every contact comes in with their city. Without geography, the list is good for sending an email and nothing else.

  4. What stays

    The list is yours, in your name and with your logins. If we stop working together tomorrow, you take it with you.

SIDE B

What holds it up.

Three tracks. Side B is the smaller side, and without it side A doesn't play.

B1THE CRATE

Eight pieces, and you flip through them like records.

What gets implemented. The first three are specific to the industry and the five after them are the foundation, which always goes in and holds up the other three.

  1. 01Fan Map

    The map of where the real people are, built on your own list.

  2. 02Booking Radar

    The Fan Map cross-checked with listening signals, territories, venues and promoters, to see which markets are worth working and with whom.

  3. 03Track Finder

    You put in a reference and get similar tracks back. A working tool for building sets and digging for music.

  4. 04CRM

    On top of the one you have. If there isn't one, it gets set up around how the project actually runs.

  5. 05Your list, organized

    One row per person and not per interaction, which is how it usually comes.

  6. 06Your own newsletter

    Your list to announce before anyone else, without paying a platform to reach your own people.

  7. 07Your own editable website

    The project's site, with the shows and the catalog, that updates itself.

  8. 08A chatbot that answers

    Answers the usual questions and hands off what it doesn't know with the whole conversation attached.
